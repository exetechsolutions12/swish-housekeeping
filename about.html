About Swish

A small business with a big personality.

Swish is run by Sarah and built around a simple idea: give people practical help in a way that feels personal, friendly and genuinely useful.

Sarah’s approach

We are dedicated to you.

Swish offers a personal service for people in all walks of life. The team can help with housework, general and commercial cleaning, ironing and errands — but Sarah has always seen the job as more than simply getting through a cleaning list.

For some people, having a friendly, familiar person visit matters just as much as the practical help. Swish housekeepers are happy to chat, spend a little time together and offer companionship alongside the work.

The result is a service that is flexible, human and built around what each person actually needs.
