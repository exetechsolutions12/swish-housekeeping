Our story

Built locally, one customer at a time.

Swish is an independent Dawlish business started by Sarah in 2014. It began with Sarah working alone and has grown into a team of 14 employed staff.

From Sarah to the Swishers

Still personal, just much bigger.

Swish has grown because the business has stayed focused on people. The team works with busy households, older and vulnerable customers, businesses, community organisations and plenty of customers who simply want housework taken off their list.

The aim is not to feel like a franchise or a faceless cleaning company. Swish is local, independent and hands-on, with Sarah still at the heart of the business.

How Swish works

  • DBS checked team members.
  • Professional cleaning products with COSHH training and data sheets.
  • Insured and trained for the work carried out.
  • Confidentiality and customer privacy taken seriously.
  • Professional products chosen with animal safety in mind.
  • All Swish staff are employed by the business.
