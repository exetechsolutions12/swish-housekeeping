About Swish

A Dawlish business with Sarah at the heart of it.

Sarah started Swish in 2014. What began with Sarah working on her own has grown into a team of 14 employed Swishers helping homes, businesses and people across the local community.

Local, independent and hands-on.

Swish has grown by looking after people properly. The team works with busy households, older and vulnerable customers, businesses, community organisations and plenty of people who simply want housework taken off their list.

It is not a franchise or a faceless cleaning company. Sarah still runs the business locally, and the team takes pride in being part of the community it works in.

The standards behind the smiles

  • DBS checked team members.
  • Professional cleaning products with COSHH training and data sheets.
  • Insured and trained for the work carried out.
  • Confidentiality and customer privacy taken seriously.
  • Professional products chosen with animal safety in mind.
  • All Swish staff are employed by the business.
