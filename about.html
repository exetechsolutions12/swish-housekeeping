About Swish

Started by Sarah. Grown in Dawlish.

Swish began in 2014 with Sarah working on her own. Today it is a team of 14 employed Swishers helping homes, businesses and people across the local community.

Still independent

A local business that stayed personal as it grew.

Swish works with busy households, older and vulnerable customers, local businesses, community organisations and plenty of people who simply want housework taken off their list.

It is not a franchise. Sarah still runs the business locally and the team is employed by Swish.

The personality is part of it too. The pink uniforms, the community events and the sense of humour are very much Swish — but customers' homes, privacy and trust are taken seriously.
