Our story

Small business. Personal service.

Swish is run by Sarah and built around one simple idea: helping people in a useful, friendly and genuinely personal way.

The people matter as much as the polish.
Why Swish is different

Not just housekeepers.

Sarah describes Swish as a small business that offers a personal service to people in all walks of life. The team carries out housework, general cleaning, commercial cleaning and ironing, while also offering companionship and practical errands.

That means a visit can be about more than getting jobs done. For some people, a chat, a little company or a helping hand with everyday life matters just as much.
