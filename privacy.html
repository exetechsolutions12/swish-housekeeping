Privacy

Privacy information

How information sent through this website is handled.

Contact information

If you contact Swish Housekeeping through the website, by phone or by email, the information you provide is used to respond to your enquiry and discuss or arrange services.

For questions about your information, contact swishhousekeeping@outlook.com or 07592 238 595.

Website enquiries

The contact form sends your enquiry directly through Formspree, the service used to process website submissions. You do not need to open an email application.

The form collects your name, email address, optional phone number, the service you are interested in and your message. Please only include information needed for your enquiry, and avoid sending passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information.

Formspree processes and stores submissions so they can be delivered to Swish Housekeeping by email. Information may therefore be held in Formspree and in the email correspondence relating to your enquiry. You can read more in Formspree's privacy policy.

Accessibility preferences

If you use the accessibility controls, your display choices are saved in your browser's local storage so they can be remembered when you move between pages or return to the website. These preferences are not sent to Swish Housekeeping. Use Reset in the accessibility panel to return to the default display.

Embedded content

The website includes content embedded from Facebook. Loading or interacting with this content may allow Facebook to process technical information and use cookies or similar technologies according to its own privacy and cookie policies.

Updates

This information will be updated when the website's forms, services or handling of information changes.
