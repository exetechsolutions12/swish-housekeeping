Ask Sarah
Companionship visits

Time, company and a little practical help.

A companionship visit can be as simple as a cup of tea and a chat, reading the paper, a board game, reminiscing or pottering in the garden. If useful, the same visit can include small household jobs such as changing the bed, putting a wash on or preparing a light snack or lunch.

The service can also give family members reassurance that someone friendly has checked in. The visit is shaped around the person, not a fixed list of tasks.
