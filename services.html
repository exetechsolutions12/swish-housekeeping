Services

How Swish can help.

From regular cleaning to companionship visits and practical home help, Swish supports people, homes and businesses in different ways.

Companionship visits

Company, conversation and a little practical help.

Visits can include a cup of tea and a chat, reading the paper, a board game, reminiscing, pottering in the garden or simply spending some friendly time together.

If useful, the same visit can include small household jobs such as changing the bed, putting a wash on or preparing a light snack or lunch. It can also give family members reassurance that someone friendly has checked in.

At home

Housekeeping & general cleaning

Regular cleaning and household support to keep your home comfortable and cared for.

Ironing

Ironing can be completed for you and returned, making one less job to think about.

Errands & shopping

Shopping, collections, drop-offs and other practical errands can be arranged where suitable.

Home help

Practical help with everyday tasks such as bed changing, washing, light meal preparation or getting shopping safely into the house.

Businesses & other spaces

Commercial cleaning

Cleaning for offices, business units, salons, community buildings and other workplaces.

Holiday lets & specialist spaces

The team also works across holiday lets and less usual settings. Recent jobs have included a theatre, village hall, farmhouses and production locations.
