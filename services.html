Companionship visits

Sometimes the most useful thing is simply having someone there.

Swish companionship visits are relaxed and shaped around the person. That might mean a cup of tea and a chat, reading the paper, a board game, reminiscing or pottering in the garden.

The visit can also include practical jobs if useful.

  • Changing the bed or putting a wash on.
  • Preparing a light snack or lunch.
  • Helping with shopping or putting it away.
  • Checking in and giving family members reassurance.
